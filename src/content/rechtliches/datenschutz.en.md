## The main point first

**What you enter into a tool on daxolo.ch does not leave your browser.**

All calculations run on your device. IBANs, amounts, reference numbers and every other
input are not transmitted, not stored and not logged. There is no server that could
receive them: daxolo consists of static files that your browser loads and then runs
itself.

This is not a promise you have to trust — it is a property of how the site is built.
You can verify it by opening your browser's developer tools and watching the network
traffic while you use a tool.

## What arises when you visit

For a website to appear at all, your browser has to request it. In doing so it passes
technically necessary information to the hosting service:

- IP address
- date and time of the request
- address requested
- browser and operating system identification

This information arises with every internet connection and serves to deliver the site
and to protect against attacks and overload. We do not retrieve it individually and do
not combine it with other data.

**Hosting:** the site is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) via a globally distributed
network. Your request is generally answered from a location in Europe. Transmission to
the USA is possible. Cloudflare is bound by standard contractual clauses and processes
the data as our processor. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the secure and
reliable operation of the site.

## Visitor numbers

We collect anonymous access statistics in order to see which tools are being used. For
this we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. This measurement works without a script on your
device, without a cookie and without any identifier that makes you recognisable.
Individual people cannot be identified and cannot be tracked across visits.

## Cookies

daxolo sets no cookies. There is therefore no consent banner either.

## What your browser stores locally

Your choice between the light and dark appearance is kept in your browser's storage so
that it is retained on your next visit. This information stays on your device and is
not transmitted. You can delete it by removing the site data in your browser.

## No third parties

daxolo loads no fonts, scripts, images or other content from external servers. There is
no advertising, no tracking pixels, no embedded maps, videos or social media elements.
This is enforced technically by a Content Security Policy that prevents connections to
external servers.

## Your rights

Under Swiss data protection law you have the right to information about, correction,
deletion and release of your data, as well as the right to object to processing. As we
process no personal data beyond the technical access data named above, information can
